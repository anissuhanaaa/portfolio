Bitcoin Project: Frequency Push and ECO

Final team project (team of six), PSDC Physical Design Program

A Bitcoin-inspired design (serial-to-parallel conversion, SRAM and a simplified SHA-256 block) was taken through physical implementation in Fusion Compiler by a team of six.

  • Started from a 1 GHz baseline, with frequency sweeps run and three targets shortlisted (1.45, 1.55 and 1.63 GHz)
  • 1.55 GHz met its target after manual ECO
  • RTL ECO done, with Formality used to confirm logical equivalence
  • Frequency and recipe optimisation was the area handled in this project

Baseline 1.00 GHz, final selected frequency 1.55 GHz with the target met after manual ECO.

ANN Diagnostic Model for Watermelon Ripeness

Final Year Project, Jul 2026

An Artificial Neural Network was built in MATLAB to grade yellow flesh watermelon ripeness, because cutting or tapping the fruit is slow and depends on the person.

  • Grades A and B classified from Visible (751 nm) and Near Infrared (1058 nm) reflectance data
  • Three training functions (traingd, traingdm and traingda) compared across 14 hidden neuron settings
  • Models judged with sensitivity, specificity and accuracy

Best result with traingda: 83.3% accuracy, 86.7% sensitivity, 80.0% specificity.

  • MATLAB
